About

Patriot HackFest is George Mason University's premier hackathon, organized by PatriotHacks, CS Club and HackOverflow. Over one weekend, students team up to design and build projects, learn new tools, and meet people from every discipline. Whether you are writing your first line of code or have shipped many projects, you are welcome to join, learn from others, and make something new.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email us at directors@patriothackfest.com

Who can participate?

Anyone can participate.

How much does it cost?

Attending is free.

How big can teams be?

Teams are 1 to 4 people. You may also compete solo.

What should I bring?

Since Patriot HackFest Fall 2026 is virtual, bring a charged laptop, your friends, and drinks to stay hydrated.

What can I build?

Anything that pertains to the track. Patriot HackFest has one track, to be announced!

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners are welcome, and there will be people around to help.
